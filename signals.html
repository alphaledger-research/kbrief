After the filing

How share prices moved after each kind of filing, measured from the close on the filing day. Compare raw returns with returns relative to the KOSDAQ or KOSPI index, over 1, 5 or 20 trading days.

Early data. Each filing joins these numbers once enough trading days have passed, so the sample grows every day. Groups with fewer than 10 filings are shown faded; treat them as anecdotes, not evidence. Past moves do not predict future ones.

Filings come from DART, Korea's official disclosure system, and prices from Yahoo Finance. Each summary is machine-generated from the original filing, with numbers quoted as filed and a link to the source.

Facts only, not investment advice. Check the original filing before acting on anything here.
