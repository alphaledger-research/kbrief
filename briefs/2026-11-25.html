  • Phase 3b, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multicenter trial of Neuronata-R (autologous bone marrow-derived mesenchymal stem cells) in adult ALS patients; to be conducted in Korea at Hanyang University Hospital and 2 other hospitals.
  • Primary objective: evaluate efficacy of two doses of Neuronata-R versus placebo in slowing functional decline. Secondary objectives include respiratory function, time to clinically important disease progression and biomarkers.
  • Dosing on Day 1 and Day 27, placebo-controlled through Week 24, then Neuronata-R for both arms at Weeks 24 and 28; assessments continue to Week 48; long-term follow-up up to 5 years after last dose.
  • Filing date 2026-11-25 is the date the IND was submitted to MFDS; the company will disclose promptly upon approval. Trial runs about 36 months from MFDS and IRB approval.
