What Pro adds

Alerts that arrive before the evening brief

Korean companies file all day. Pro checks DART every few minutes during market hours and sends the English summary of any filing from your watchlist as soon as it is ready.

Overhang tracker

Convertible bond filings state how many shares the new bond can turn into and, often, the total from every convertible still outstanding. Pro keeps that running total for each company, shows it against shares outstanding and flags conversion price cuts, the most common source of surprise dilution in KOSDAQ small caps.

Alerts you define

Combine a sector, a filing type and a size threshold, and get only the filings that match, for example every semiconductor equipment contract worth more than 20% of the company's market cap.

Data for your own research

Every summary with its numbers, the market cap at filing and the 1, 5 and 20-day price reaction, as CSV or through an API.

Why this exists

Since May 2026, KOSPI companies with assets of KRW 2 trillion or more must publish key filings in English, and the rule is set to cover every KOSPI company from March 2027. KOSDAQ companies have no such rule, so most of them file only in Korean while foreign investors trade a growing share of the market. This service turns those filings into plain English, ranks them by size against the company, quotes every number as filed and links each summary to its source. How it works.

Filings come from DART, Korea's official disclosure system, and prices from Yahoo Finance. Each summary is machine-generated from the original filing, with numbers quoted as filed and a link to the source.

Facts only, not investment advice. Check the original filing before acting on anything here.
